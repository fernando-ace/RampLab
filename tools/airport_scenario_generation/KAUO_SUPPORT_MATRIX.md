# KAUO support and accuracy matrix

| Category | Modeled content | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| AUTHORITATIVE | FAA NASR KAUO airport reference point, field elevation, runway 18/36 and 11/29 dimensions, and all four surveyed runway-end latitude/longitude records | October 1, 2026 NASR subscription, cited in `tools/airport_data_ingestion/examples/kauo/PROVENANCE.md`; FAA lists runway-end coordinates as third-party survey positions dated May 22, 2006, without a horizontal accuracy estimate. |
| AUTHORITATIVE chart facts | Runway/taxiway labels, named airport areas, published runway surface/dimensions, charted relative layout | Current-effective FAA airport diagram cited in provenance. Its drawing is not a surveyed taxiway centerline or apron coordinate dataset. |
| Real / public observation | Auburn University airport states full-service FBO, fuels, and flight-training operators | Official airport website; categories only, no individual flight or equipment observation. |
| DERIVED | WGS-84-to-ENU conversion; runway centers/headings and intersection from FAA runway ends; runway length comparison; graph edge lengths from endpoint coordinates | Calculated deterministically by `geospatial.py` and `calibrate_kauo.py`; errors and source coordinates are in `kauo_alignment_validation.json`. |
| APPROXIMATE | Taxiway graph, apron, terminal/hangar, fuel-farm, and parking/stand coordinates | Goal 25 diagram-digitized relative anchors migrated to ENU; not surveyed centerlines, pavement polygons, or published stand coordinates. |
| Assumed | One representative GA arrival/departure pair, aircraft archetype, operation timestamps, abstract pushback-preparation task and its two-minute duration, taxi speed 5 m/s, rollout/runway occupancy timings | Scenario-exercise inputs. They are neither actual KAUO movements nor airport performance measurements. |
| Unsupported | Event-level flight history, actual runway choice, real taxi track/time, real parking assignment, aircraft registration/model, collision-suitable aircraft envelope, equipment inventory/spawn, service duration/distribution, live NOTAM/closure, surveyed road/pavement geometry | Not obtained from public sources for this prototype. |
| Not applicable to the sample | Airline gates, passenger terminal bridges, baggage handling, catering, cabin cleaning, commercial airline turnaround schedule | Not inferred from the airport's FBO services or the simulator's available task types. |

The runway layer is geographically calibrated to FAA runway-end coordinates within the reported numerical tolerance, while taxiway/apron geometry remains approximate. The result supports only a **KAUO-calibrated simulation prototype** based on limited public static data. FBO fuel availability is public, but simulated fueling is unsupported because equipment inventory, dispatch locations, and service durations are unknown. The abstract pushback-preparation task is a scenario input, not an observed ground operation. It does not support an operational digital-twin or safety-validation claim.

The generated runs have one aircraft and no ground vehicles. Their zero collision counts are observed for those modeled actors, but aircraft-aircraft, aircraft-ground, and fleet minimum-separation fields have no actor pair to measure; exported `0.0` separation values must be reported as **not applicable**, not as zero-meter clearance.
