# Goal45 KAUO source register

Research checked **2026-10-14**. This register distinguishes published facts from coordinate geometry. A source can establish a label, pavement use, or project proposal without establishing an operational centerline.

## Findings

The strongest public source for exact operational coordinates found is the FAA 28 Day NASR APT CSV, effective **2026-10-01**. It supplies the airport reference position, runway identities and dimensions, and four runway-end positions. FAA source fields identify third-party surveys dated **2006-05-22**. Those fields provide no horizontal accuracy estimate. The committed extract retains all columns for the AUO rows from `APT_BASE.csv`, `APT_RWY.csv`, and `APT_RWY_END.csv`; the source archive and member hashes are recorded in [`source-artifact-manifest.json`](goal45-evidence/source-artifact-manifest.json).

The current FAA Airport Diagram (cycle 2610, current when checked) establishes chart labels and relative layout, including Taxiways A, B, C, A1–A4, C2 and C3, the terminal/FBO apron, west apron, north hangar complex, and self-serve fuel farm. It depicts hold markings and warns that runway crossing clearances are required. It does not provide surveyed centerline coordinates, current stand coordinates, service roads, vehicle staging points, or crossing permissions. A diagram digitized into coordinates remains approximate.

The Auburn University Board packet drawing is a useful historical layout reference, not a current as-built or georeferenced dataset. The April 2024 terminal-ramp expansion packet describes proposed tie-down/apron work. ALDOT's June 2026 grant table lists north terminal-area apron phases 5 and 6. Neither establishes that the work is complete or supplies surveyed operational coordinates.

ALDOT's Auburn pavement report (final report dated February 2022; November 2019 visual inspection) inventories 19 pavement branches and 31 sections and reports areas, pavement use, condition and program recommendations. It is not a geospatial centerline or stand dataset. The ALDOT GIS viewer and its associated report were checked; no AUO operational layer with suitable geometry accuracy and permissions was obtained.

Auburn Facilities Management states that project plans and specifications are provided through its Public Works Bid Calendar after contractor enrollment and project prequalification. This identifies a plausible route to current engineering drawings, but those project documents were not accessed in this review. The current FAA-approved Airport Layout Plan and its revision date were not publicly obtained.

## Geometry supported by the current canonical dataset

- **AUTHORITATIVE:** 1 FAA airport reference point and 4 FAA runway-end points. Their source attribution is strong; horizontal accuracy remains unknown.
- **SOURCE_DERIVED:** 2 straight runway centerlines connecting the published threshold points, with widths and published lengths. These are derived axes, not runway pavement polygons.
- **APPROXIMATE:** 54 old reference nodes and 94 old graph links, held in the rejected collection for review only. None are included in production routing.
- **SYNTHETIC:** 0 canonical operational geometries.
- **Missing canonical geometry:** taxiway centerlines/intersections, taxilanes, current apron boundaries, aircraft stands, service roads, service areas, GSE staging points, hold-short positions and runway crossing features/permissions.

The missing categories are not silently filled from imagery, nearest-pavement snapping, or chart digitization. The exact request is in [`goal45-kauo-data-request.md`](goal45-kauo-data-request.md).
