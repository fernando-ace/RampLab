Subject: Request for current georeferenced KAUO airfield geometry and access data

Hello,

I am developing RampLab, a research simulator, and am looking for current, georeferenced geometry and operational access information for Auburn University Regional Airport (KAUO). If shareable, could you provide an existing GIS/CAD/survey export or advise which data custodian should be contacted?

The most useful items would be runway thresholds and pavement boundaries or widths; taxiway/taxilane centerlines, intersections and hold-short locations; apron/ramp boundaries; aircraft stand locations and access/pushback links; service roads and GSE staging/access areas; runway crossing permissions; restricted areas and operational constraints; and the widths/boundaries needed for clearance checks.

GeoJSON/GIS, Shapefile/GeoPackage, georeferenced CAD/DXF, or surveyed point/edge CSV would work. Please include the CRS/datum, units, revision or survey date, source feature IDs, accuracy/precision statement, and any use or redistribution limits. Proposed, approximate, or non-current data is still useful if clearly identified, but will not be treated as operationally authoritative.
